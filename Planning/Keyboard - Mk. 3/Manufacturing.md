OEM - OEM stands for ==Original Equipment Manufacturer==. It refers to a company that produces components or products that are then incorporated into another company's finished product, often under the branding of that second company. In essence, OEMs are suppliers to other manufacturers.

# Case
- Aluminum Top Case
- Aluminum Bottom Case
- **Plate** - Polycarbonate, POM, or FR4
	- Gasket mounted
- PCB Foam
	- 3.5mm poron foam between plate and PCB
- Case foam
	- Thin poron foam with cutouts for the hotswap sockets on top (touching PCB)
	- Silicone or EVA layer below that for further sound reduction 
- Countersunk screws (M3) from bottom of case into top case threaded holes
- Top case is a flat rectangle that partially wraps the bottom case
- Bottom case has 6.5º angle to it
- Front of case ~17mm tall
- Bottom and Top cases are flush at the front
