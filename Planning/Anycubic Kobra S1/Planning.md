- Honest Review
	- How honest can it really be when a company sends you a free printer, so I'll do my best to ignore that bias


# Pros
- Plug and play, literally in every way
- Multi-material/multi-color is amazing
- The RFID scanning of their filaments is awesome. It's also super easy to set up new filaments both on the printer and in the anycubic slicer


# Cons
- Setting up multi-color printing in the slicer is not as intuitive as it could be. I usually just print simple things like a white background with some text on it that's a different color, but that height range tool is a huge pain to work with and I can't figure out a simpler way.
