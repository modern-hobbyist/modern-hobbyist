Linseed Oil
- Is a good option since it can seal both the wood and the metal

## Finishing
Use an old t shirt or rag and wipe and after 5-15 minutes, wipe off the excess oil to prevent it from becoming a sticky gooey blob.

## Fire hazard
One thing to keep in mind when using oils (linseed or tung) is that the rags can and will combust if left balled up in a trash can. As the oil oxidizes and breaks down, it generates lots of heat that if unable to escape, will cause the rag to ignite.

- Either need to be laid flat outside until they are dry and crusty
- Store them in an old metal paint can to starve them of oxygen
- Store them in a fire safe rag bin
