## What are your favorite Obsidian Plugins?
- [Paste URL into Selection](https://github.com/denolehov/obsidian-url-into-selection) - Allows me to quickly paste in links to existing text.
- [LanguageTool Integration](https://github.com/Clemens-E/obsidian-languagetool-plugin) - Spell checker that's not annoying. 
- [Git](https://github.com/Vinzent03/obsidian-git) - Set up to auto-commit every 10m unless I do it manually.
- [Excalidraw](https://github.com/zsviczian/obsidian-excalidraw-plugin) - Not so much for Modern Hobbyist projects, but I use it ***constantly*** at work
